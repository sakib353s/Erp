What masking does

Three rules — and they are the reason a support person may read a production log.

Redacted before rendering. A password, an API token, a card number or a connection string is replaced with [masked] as the line is read, so the unmasked text never reaches the browser.
The search runs after redaction. So an empty result is not proof that a leaked key is absent — it is proof that no line contains your text once secrets were removed. Said out loud on purpose.
Read-only. Opening a log is not maintenance: nothing is written, nothing is cleared, and this page cannot delete the evidence its own maintenance runs left behind.
