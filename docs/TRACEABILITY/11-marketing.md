# 11. MARKETING — Traceability

Baseline (`BL`) applies. Spec §21: marketing = real systems with reusable contacts, segments, templates, scheduling, **delivery states**, opt-out state, reports. External platforms = clean adapters + config screen; core CRM/marketing works without them. **Never fake delivery/open/click numbers** — only provider-verified events (webhook) or locally tracked application events.

**Built this slice: the marketing desk (§11-01…§11-05, §11-08, §11-15, §11-16, §11-21, §11-22) — and the sentence the whole slice is written around is “nothing here claims a delivery it did not make”.** The four channel groups of the catalogue are *one* desk (`/app/marketing/campaigns?channel=sms|email|whatsapp|push`), not four controllers that would drift: a campaign carries its channel, and the channel decides what an address is (`phone` or `email`), what a template may say, and whether anything can actually be sent. `CampaignService::resolveTransport()` is the only place that answers that last question, and it answers it from configuration rather than from optimism: an SMS campaign is handed to the outbox **only** when the company has an active `SmsProvider` whose `config_status` is `configured`; an email campaign only when `mail.default` names a real driver (`log`, `array` and an empty string are all “no transport” — a screen is not a postman); WhatsApp and push have no adapter in this build, so their messages are written as **held** with the reason, and the desk says so in the row. There is no code path in this module that writes `sent`, which is why no test in `MarketingDeskTest` ever expects it: a message is `queued` (handed over), `not_configured` (held, with the reason on the row), or `failed` — and a failure keeps the transport's own words in `last_error` rather than a paraphrase, so the delivery lens reads like a log rather than like a brochure.

**The audience is a rule, expanded on the day, and every person it does not reach is a row with a reason.** `all | recent_buyers | dormant | manual` are stored on the campaign with `audience_days` for the windowed two, and expanded at launch — a buyer who qualified this morning is in and one who did not is out, because a member list frozen last week is a list about last week. Opt-outs are a table (`marketing_optouts`), one row per company + channel + contact, with `all` as a channel that suppresses everywhere; they are enforced **in the service** so no screen can forget them, written idempotently, sourced (`manual|reply|campaign|import`) so the desk can say where the objection came from, and lifted with a reason on the audit trail (`marketing.optout_lifted`). A skipped customer is not dropped silently: the recipient row keeps the frozen name and contact, `skip_reason` says `opted_out`, `no_address` or `blacklisted`, no message is created for them, and the counts at the top of the page and the rows underneath come from the same single pass — the same rules the dispatcher applies, in the same order.

**Cost is a price frozen per recipient; revenue is the invoices those recipients actually raised.** `cost_per_message` is copied onto each recipient row at launch, so repricing a campaign tomorrow cannot rewrite what yesterday cost. `attribution_days` names the window after `launched_at`, and the revenue figure is the total of invoices from the customers a transport was actually handed — dated inside that window, status `issued|partial|paid` — never a click, never an estimate, and never the invoice of somebody who was skipped. A launched campaign is history: it cannot be edited, it cannot be launched twice, and it can only be closed with a reason (`cancel`), which is a decision the desk records instead of a row it deletes.

**Fourteen things this build does not do are named on their own page rather than hidden.** `marketing.capabilities/{topic}` (14 topics) says what is missing, in what way, and what real screen does part of the same job instead — Meta/Google/TikTok pixels and ad accounts, open and click tracking, drip sequences, A/B testing, read receipts, a WhatsApp chat inbox, an SMS balance API, triggered messages, abandoned-cart, birthday/anniversary, Google review, social scheduling, and the three groups that have no record at all here (leads, affiliates, influencers — see below). The pages are written as small essays with a `details` list, because “not built” is only useful with the reason attached. The catalogue pins every unbuilt leaf at one of them and the settings leaves at `/app/settings/notifications`; promotions and coupons point at the **Sales** desks that already own the discount engine (02-101…02-107) rather than a second one that could disagree with the till.
