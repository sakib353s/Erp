# 08. CASH & BANK — Traceability

Baseline (`BL`) applies. Shared services: `CashBank\Actions\{OpenCashSession, CloseCashSession, RecordCashReceipt, PostCashPayment, TransferCash, ReconcileBank, ImportBankStatement, PostBankCharge, ClearCheque, BounceCheque, CreateExpense, ApproveRecurringExpense, RequestPettyCash, ApprovePettyCashRequest, ReplenishPettyCash, PostWalletTransaction}` · `CashBank\Services\{CashSessionService, ChequeService, PettyCashService, BankReconciliationService, WalletService}` · `Accounting\JournalPostingService` (all money movement), `DocumentRenderer` (receipts/cheque printouts), ApprovalEngine for expenses/payments above thresholds.

All cash/bank/wallet movement = GL postings; mobile wallets (bKash/Nagad/Rocket/Upay) are financial accounts with **manual reconciliation** (API = optional adapter, never faked).

## §08 as shipped so far

The desk, the books and the registry are one mechanism: **a money account is an
account in the chart of accounts that money can sit in**, so the registry adds
only what a ledger cannot say (which bank, which number, which provider) and
every position on the screens is the sum of posted journal lines rather than a
stored balance. `MoneyAccountService` owns the registry and the positions;
`MoneyMovementService` owns every way money moves — in, out, and between the
company's own accounts — and each movement posts through `JournalPostingService`
(the one path allowed to post) with a numbered document behind it: a money
receipt (MR), an expense voucher (EX) or a cash transfer (CT).

The two lists cannot drift apart, and that is enforced rather than assumed: the
migration backfills `instrument` from the `is_cash` / `is_bank` flags the standard
chart already carried, `MoneyAccountService` still finds a flagged account whose
column was never filled (a chart seeded *after* this migration is exactly that
case) and derives its instrument when asked, and every write to an account keeps
the column and the flags saying the same thing — a flagged account is filled in,
an instrument a screen set explicitly is left alone, and a group row is never a
money account. `CashBankDeskTest` pins the derived position; the migration and the
model hook are what keep `instrument` and the flags from ever disagreeing.

Still PLANNED here, and why: **bank reconciliation, statement import and wallet
reconciliation (08-08, 08-09, 08-12)** need a statement-line store and a matching
workflow; **cheque management and cheque print (08-13, 08-14)** need the cheque
register with its cleared/bounced postings; **expenses, categories, approval and
recurring (08-15…08-19)** need the category↔account mapping and the approval
engine wiring; **petty cash (08-21)** needs the fund and its replenishment
journal; **cash count and session variance (08-05)** needs the count sheet;
**cash/bank/expense reports (08-20, 08-22)** are the reporting family on top of
what is now posted — the per-account book exists already (08-07), the report
centre family does not.
